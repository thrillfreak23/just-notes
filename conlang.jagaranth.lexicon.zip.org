$ zip
number :: zero
===

#number #numeral

* usage:
**** as verbal root: *zipâl-* to sum to zero, to not exist
****** example :: voveps zipâlos :: Your God does not exist.
****** parse :: God.2ndSgPossessor zero.Finite.3rdSgInsapient


****** example :: î zip voveps 
****** translation :: Your god is nothing. Your god is a zero.

****** example :: zipâlos voveps
****** parse :: zero.Finite.3rsSgInsapientSubject God.2ndSgPossessor
****** translation :: Your God does not exist.
******* note :: the *\-os* says "Your God" is insapient and thus incapable of speech
