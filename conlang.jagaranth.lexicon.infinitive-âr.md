# -âr
: verbal infinitive marker :: kekâr :: to strike, hit
: NOT the act of hitting or striking :: that's with -um
: NOT the idea of hitting or striking :: that's with -othan



[-um](conlang.jagaranth.lexicon.-um.md) 
: concretizing verbal suffix :: suffixed to infinitive, expresses the action of the verb
: kekârum :: the act of striking

[-othor](conlang.jagaranth.lexicon.-othor.md) 
: abstracting verbal suffix :: suffixed to the infinitive, expresses the idea of the verb
: kekârothor :: the idea of striking

[lothor](conlang.jagaranth.lexicon.lothor.md)
: nominal :: a thought
