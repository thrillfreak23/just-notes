# homin
: nominal :: a kind of biologic sapient autochthonous to the Rorb system

- note: In subspace radio dramas, homin are stereotypically regarded as sly and
  cunning pirates, rakes, smugglers, delinquents, mercenaries, or religious
  maniacs, in descending order of occurrence in the scripts; pirates is without
  doubt the most popular hominid stereotype.
