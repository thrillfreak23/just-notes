$ -um
*concretizing verbal suffix :: suffixed to infinitive, expresses an instance the action of the verb*
*kekârum :: a blow, a strike, a hit*
nota bene :: the infinitive in -âr expresses the action of the verb
nota bene :: the infinitive with -um denotes an actual blow, not the idea of one

- note
- there is no full word ending in -um that -um comes from. it's an anomaly.
