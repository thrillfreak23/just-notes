# Jagaranth Intro

- Jagaranth is a purely agglutinative language of the Jagarôth linguistic
  family.
 
- It is the language of the Low-Canals Region of Mars, one of the more
  legendary locales in all of Galaxia, and the legendarium in question is
  replete with tales of crime, oppression, depression, adventure, and violence;
  also drug abuse abounds there, along with juvenile and adult delinquency,
  madness, dejection, abjection, prostution, religious mania, monomania; the
  people there perpetrate a gamut of addictions, heroisms, damnations and
  salvations.

- The semiautonomous city of Jarthex dominates here a landscape of convergent
  canals, manufactoria, latifundia, and low small cities that are little more
  than heaps of hovels. The country is flat red sand and boulders, with low
  hills that vanish when the dust is up, and coppices of scrannel trees that
  clash with shiny blackish leaves.

- A curious feature of Jagaranth and all the languages of the Jagarôth family
  is composability and construability, that is, the ability to combine
  morphemes with few limits other than intelligibility.
